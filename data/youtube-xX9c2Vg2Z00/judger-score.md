## Scope and scoring method

The source model provides categorical options but no numeric scale, weights, or thresholds, so this report does not calculate a total score or claim to measure debate quality. Confidence describes how strongly the supplied captions support each classification; it is not a score for the speaker.

## Overall profile

The supplied text presents a pragmatic, policy-and-figures-led speaker who follows parliamentary conventions. In adversarial exchanges, he often answers part of a question and then pivots to a counterargument. Contrast and repeated phrasing provide his clearest rhetorical patterns. This is a style classification of caption text, not an assessment of factual accuracy or debate quality.
